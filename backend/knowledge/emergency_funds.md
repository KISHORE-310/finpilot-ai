# Emergency Funds: Purpose, Sizing, and Management

An emergency fund is a dedicated pool of highly liquid savings designed strictly to absorb unexpected, urgent expenses without incurring high-interest consumer debt or liquidating long-term investments during market downturns.

### Sizing Guidelines
1. **Baseline Liquidity**: Most financial education standards recommend holding between 3 to 6 months of essential living expenses (housing, utilities, groceries, insurance, minimum debt obligations) in an accessible vehicle such as a high-yield savings account or money market account.
2. **Variable Income Consideration**: Individuals with irregular, seasonal, or freelance income typically target 6 to 9 months of expenses to buffer revenue volatility.
3. **Dual-Income vs Single-Income**: Households with a single income stream benefit from larger reserves compared to households with multiple independent earners.

### Key Rules
- Keep funds separate from daily checking accounts to prevent incidental spending.
- Prioritize liquidity and capital preservation over aggressive investment yield.
- Replenish the reserve promptly after drawing down for an eligible unexpected expense.
