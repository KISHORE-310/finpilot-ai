# Structured Debt Reduction Strategies

Managing and eliminating high-interest consumer debt is a critical prerequisite for building sustainable net worth.

### Debt Avalanche Method (Mathematically Optimal)
- Rank all debts in descending order by Interest Rate (APR).
- Pay minimum balances on all accounts.
- Direct all extra debt-repayment capital toward the debt with the highest interest rate.
- **Advantage**: Minimizes total cumulative interest paid and reduces overall time in debt.

### Debt Snowball Method (Behaviorally Motivated)
- Rank debts in ascending order by Total Balance Size regardless of interest rate.
- Direct extra payments toward the smallest balance until fully eliminated, then roll that payment into the next smallest.
- **Advantage**: Provides rapid psychological victories that help sustain momentum.

### Debt-to-Income (DTI) Benchmark
A consumer DTI below 36% (with housing under 28%) is generally considered manageable. DTI exceeding 43% often restricts borrowing capacity and signals elevated debt burden.
