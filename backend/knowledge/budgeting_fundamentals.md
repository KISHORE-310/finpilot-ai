# Budgeting Methodologies: 50/30/20 and Zero-Based Allocation

A budget is a forward-looking cash flow plan that aligns monthly income with recurring expenses, discretionary spending, and long-term wealth accumulation.

### The 50/30/20 Rule
- **50% Needs**: Obligatory essentials including rent/mortgage, groceries, utilities, basic transportation, minimum debt payments, and healthcare.
- **30% Wants**: Discretionary expenditures such as dining out, recreation, travel, subscriptions, and non-essential shopping.
- **20% Savings & Debt Acceleration**: Principal debt reduction above minimums, emergency fund contributions, retirement accounts, and investment accounts.

### Zero-Based Budgeting
In a zero-based budget, every dollar of net income is deliberately allocated to an expense, savings target, or debt category before the month begins ($	ext{Income} - 	ext{Outflows} = 0$).

### Managing Budget Overruns
When spending in a category exceeds planned limits:
1. Reallocate unspent funds from discretionary categories to maintain net savings targets.
2. Review recurring subscriptions and variable spending patterns for consolidation.
